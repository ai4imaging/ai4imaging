# PKU Computational Scientific Imaging Lab!

**Welcome to the Computational Scientific Imaging Lab (CSI Lab) at Peking University!**

Our lab pioneers research at the intersection of computational imaging, computer science, and physical sciences. We develop advanced algorithms and innovative hardware to push the boundaries of scientific imaging across various disciplines, including biomedicine and astrophysics.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus

Our work is inherently interdisciplinary, combining expertise in:
- **Computational Imaging**
- **Deep Learning & AI**
- **Computer Vision & Graphics**
- **Statistical Inference**
- **Hardware Design**

We aim to facilitate future scientific imaging at extreme scales through cutting-edge computational methods.
